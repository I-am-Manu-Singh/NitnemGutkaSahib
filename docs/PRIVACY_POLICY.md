### 1. Introduction & Spiritual Reverence
Nitnem Steek is dedicated to serving the global Sikh Sangat with an authentic, respectful, and distraction-free digital Gurbani companion. We firmly uphold user privacy and transparency as paramount core values. 

This Privacy Policy explains how our application collects, uses, and safeguards your information when you interact with Nitnem Gutka Steek.

---

### 2. Core Philosophy: Offline-First & Privacy by Design
* **100% Offline-First Gurbani Core:** All Gurmukhi Gurbani texts, Steek translations (English, Punjabi, and Spanish), transliterations, and essential audio files are bundled locally on your device.
* **No Tracking of Scripture Reading:** Your personal reading, listening, recitation history, bookmarks, and Nitnem habits remain strictly stored on your local device. We do not track or log what Banis or Shabads you read or listen to.

---

### 3. Information We Collect
We collect the absolute minimum data necessary to provide essential cloud features:

1. **Spiritual Milestones & Backup (Optional):**
   * If you optionally choose to sign in (via Google Firebase Authentication), we store non-sensitive spiritual milestones—such as your total XP, active streak count, completed Banis for the day, and Sangat group leaderboard standings—in Cloud Firestore so your progress is preserved when switching or reinstalling devices.
   * If you choose not to sign in, all progress data remains exclusively on your local device.
2. **In-App Purchases & Voluntary Seva Support:**
   * In-app purchases, subscriptions, and voluntary Seva patron memberships are securely processed directly by the **Google Play Store** (via RevenueCat billing infrastructure).
   * **We never receive, access, or store your credit card, debit card, bank account, or financial details.** RevenueCat and Google Play process purchase receipts and provide us solely with anonymous entitlement tokens to grant your tier status.
3. **Crash Diagnostics & Performance (Anonymous):**
   * Non-identifiable diagnostic metrics and crash logs may be collected through standard Google Play Vitals to identify bugs and ensure uninterrupted media playback across varying device hardware.

---

### 4. Zero Advertising & Third-Party Data Selling
* **Zero Third-Party Advertising:** Nitnem Gutka Steek has **zero** third-party ad networks (e.g., Google AdMob, Meta Audience Network). The app is 100% ad-free forever.
* **No Selling of Personal Data:** We never sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal data, spiritual journey, or account credentials to any data brokers or advertisers.

---

### 5. Device Permissions & Purpose
Nitnem Gutka Steek requests only essential permissions strictly aligned with app functionality:
* `POST_NOTIFICATIONS`: Solely utilized to deliver scheduled Amritvela, Rehras Sahib, and Kirtan Sohila Nitnem reminders configured by you.
* `FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK`: Required by Android system architecture to maintain uninterrupted audio playback of Nitnem Banis while your screen is turned off or while using other applications.

---

### 6. Data Retention & Account Deletion
You maintain complete ownership of your data:
* You can reset or wipe all local app data directly from **Settings > Privacy & Data Management > Clear Local Data**.
* If you have an active cloud account and wish to permanently erase all associated cloud records (email, streak history, leaderboard profile), you can request immediate deletion directly in-app via the support channel or by emailing **[creativeblisslabs@gmail.com](mailto:creativeblisslabs@gmail.com)**. Upon request, all associated cloud records are permanently purged within 30 days.

Nitnem Gutka Steek contains sacred spiritual content suitable for all ages and families. We do not knowingly solicit or collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13.
